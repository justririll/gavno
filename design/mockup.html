Click the screen so the keys work. In a browser the system often grabs Super, so Alt works as Super here. Drag windows by the title bar to the edges, hover the maximize button, drag an empty area sideways or up. The dock magnifies under the pointer; the minimize button sends a window into its dock icon, and a click on the icon brings it back.

Gestures

  • Three fingers sideways: adjacent workspace, motion follows the fingers 1:1. Here that is dragging an empty area or horizontal touchpad scroll.
  • Three fingers up: overview, down: back. Release halfway and the spring finishes at the same speed.
  • In the overview, a window can be dragged onto a workspace thumbnail at the top or onto an adjacent workspace.
